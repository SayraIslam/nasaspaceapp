# NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026
"Base 0” is an interactive, educational space game where players experience the systems thinking, resource management, scientific reasoning, and trade-offs behind human space exploration. Set at a single lunar outpost, the game puts players in charge of critical decisions, supported by AI assistants, so they learn to think and tinker like engineers.

Built on research into NASA’s work on outer space, the game packages months’ worth of knowledge into a fun, hands-on experience that goes beyond what reading alone can offer. Players balance limited resources, weigh competing priorities, and see the consequences of their choices, just as real mission planners do.

Our goal is to make space science accessible, engaging, and memorable, inspiring the next generation to build humanity’s future in space.
